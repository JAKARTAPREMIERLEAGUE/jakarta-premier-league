What's new in Season 3

A bigger match-day experience.

We are planning new ways to make the tournament more engaging for players, supporters and families.

01 / NIGHT CRICKET

Floodlit matches

A night-match atmosphere with ground lighting, a lively crowd and a premium event feel.

Planned feature
02 / REVIEW SYSTEM

DRS-style match reviews

Planned third-umpire reviews for LBW, run-outs and stumpings, subject to the final tournament rules and technology setup.

Planned feature
03 / BROADCAST

Live streaming and replays

Multi-camera coverage, match replays and slow-motion moments, with a large LED scoreboard experience.

Planned feature
04 / FAN EXPERIENCE

Food and community festival

A welcoming event atmosphere for players, families and spectators beyond the boundary.

Planned feature
05 / MORE SPORTS

Activities beyond cricket

We are exploring additional activities such as badminton and family-friendly games alongside the cricket event.

Under planning
06 / RECOGNITION

Celebrate every contribution

More opportunities to recognise players, teams, volunteers, partners and the people who make JPL possible.

Planning concept
More than a tournament

Cricket brings us together.

Season 3 is being planned as a community event — a place to compete, connect, cheer, discover new activities and celebrate the growth of cricket in Indonesia.

For playersCompetitive tennis-ball cricket, team spirit and memorable match-day moments.
For families and fansA welcoming environment with activities and entertainment beyond the game.
For the cricket communityA platform to connect clubs, supporters, sponsors and aspiring players.
