A national picture

Cricket is played beyond Jakarta

Indonesia's cricket landscape includes national development, provincial activity, club competitions and grassroots programmes. Persatuan Cricket Indonesia (PCI) describes its work as extending across the country, with more than 22 provincial committees and activity reaching from Aceh to Papua.

For official information about national cricket development, provincial activity and the sport's recognised structures, readers should refer to PCI and the International Cricket Council (ICC) profile for Indonesia.

Cricket infrastructure · North Bali

Bali International Oval: a major step for the game

The Bali International Oval in Desa Sanggalangit, Kecamatan Gerokgak, Buleleng, is an ambitious new cricket facility being developed in North Bali. Planned as a two-oval complex on a site of approximately five hectares, it is intended to support elite training as well as domestic and international cricket.

The wider vision includes high-performance training through the Garuda National Cricket Academy, stronger preparation pathways for Indonesian teams, and the capacity to welcome visiting teams and major competitions.

PCI Chairman Abhiram Singh Yadav has publicly supported the venue's potential to help cricket grow in Bali and across Indonesia. Local authorities have also discussed its use for Porprov Bali 2027.

A proud moment for Indonesian cricket

Congratulations to PCI Chairman Abhiram Singh Yadav

On 24 September 2026, the International Cricket Council announced that Abhiram Singh Yadav of Persatuan Cricket Indonesia had been elected to an Associate Member position on the ICC Chief Executives' Committee (CEC). He will serve until the next elections at the ICC Annual Conference in June 2027.

At Jakarta Premier League, we are proud to see Indonesian cricket leadership represented at this important international level. We congratulate Mr Yadav on this milestone and wish him every success as he contributes to the continued growth of the game.

Common questions

Cricket in Indonesia: FAQs

Helpful starting points for people learning about cricket in Indonesia and JPL's place in the local cricket community.

Who is the national cricket governing body in Indonesia?

Persatuan Cricket Indonesia (PCI) identifies itself as Indonesia's official cricket governing body. Visit PCI and the ICC member profile for official national-level information.

Is cricket played outside Jakarta?

Yes. PCI describes cricket development through provincial committees across Indonesia, from Aceh to Papua. Its website is the best place to check the latest regional information.

What is Jakarta Premier League?

JPL is a Jakarta-based tournament organiser and community platform focused on tennis-ball cricket, local competitions and bringing players and supporters together.
