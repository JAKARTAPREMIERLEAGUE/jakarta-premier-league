The game across the archipelago

Cricket in Indonesia

Cricket connects players, clubs, schools and local communities across Indonesia. From organised club competitions in Jakarta to the wider development of the game across the provinces, the cricket story is bigger than one city or one tournament.

A national picture

Cricket is played beyond Jakarta

Indonesia's cricket landscape includes national development, provincial activity, club competitions and grassroots programmes. Persatuan Cricket Indonesia (PCI) describes its work as extending across the country, with more than 22 provincial committees and activity reaching from Aceh to Papua.

For official information about national cricket development, provincial activity and the sport's recognised structures, readers should refer to PCI and the International Cricket Council (ICC) profile for Indonesia.

Jakarta Premier League: tournaments and community

Jakarta Premier League (JPL) is a Jakarta-based cricket tournament organiser and community platform. We focus on bringing teams, players, families and supporters together through tennis-ball cricket tournaments and shared match-day experiences. JPL is not the national governing body for cricket in Indonesia; our role is to organise local tournaments and help strengthen the community around the game.

Common questions

Cricket in Indonesia: FAQs

Helpful starting points for people learning about cricket in Indonesia and JPL's place in the local cricket community.

Who is the national cricket governing body in Indonesia?

Persatuan Cricket Indonesia (PCI) identifies itself as Indonesia's official cricket governing body. Visit PCI and the ICC member profile for official national-level information.

Is cricket played outside Jakarta?

Yes. PCI describes cricket development through provincial committees across Indonesia, from Aceh to Papua. Its website is the best place to check the latest regional information.

What is Jakarta Premier League?

JPL is a Jakarta-based tournament organiser and community platform focused on tennis-ball cricket, local competitions and bringing players and supporters together.

How can a team get involved with JPL?

Follow JPL's Season 3 page for team-registration information, or contact the organising team at jakartapremierleague50@gmail.com.
